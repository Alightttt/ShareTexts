How to Transfer Files Between Devices

Move text, photos, videos, and files between any two devices directly from your browser. No app to install. No account to create.

The short answer: Open sharetexts.online on both devices. Click Send on the first, enter the code on the second. Select your file. It arrives.

Step by Step

  1. Open ShareText on both devices

    Visit sharetexts.online in any browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge. Works on phones, tablets, and computers.

  2. Connect the two devices

    On the device that has the file, click Send something. You'll see a 6-digit pairing code. On the other device, click Receive something and enter that code. Or scan the QR code.

  3. Transfer your file

    Type text, paste a link, or tap the attachment button to select a file. The transfer starts immediately. When it's done, the other device shows the received content with a Save button.

What You Can Transfer

Text, links, and code — Paste anything. It appears on the other device instantly.

Photos and videos — Select images or videos from your camera roll or file system.

Any file — Documents, PDFs, ZIPs, audio, and more. No size limit imposed by ShareText.

Supported Devices

ShareText works on any two devices with a modern browser and an internet connection:

  • iPhone and Android phones
  • Windows, macOS, and Linux computers
  • iPads and Android tablets
  • Any combination — iPhone to Windows, Android to Mac, phone to tablet, and more

How It Stays Private

ShareText connects two devices directly using encrypted browser technology. Your files move between devices, not through a cloud server. Temporary rooms close automatically. No accounts. No history.
