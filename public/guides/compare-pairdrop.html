ShareTexts vs PairDrop

Both are browser-based file transfer tools. PairDrop focuses on local network discovery. ShareTexts adds pairing codes and text transfer.

ShareTexts
Pairing-code based. Works across any network. Adds text transfer, share links, and QR codes.
PairDrop
Self-hosted or public instance. Discovers devices on the same network. File transfer only.

Where ShareTexts Is Useful

  • Cross-network transfers — devices on different Wi-Fi networks, different locations
  • Text transfer — links, code, notes between devices
  • QR/pairing codes — no need for devices to discover each other
  • Guest devices — works on any device with a browser

Where PairDrop Is Better

  • Self-hosting — full control over your transfer infrastructure
  • Local network speed — direct LAN transfer can be faster
  • Offline — works without internet (same network only)
  • Auto-discovery — no codes needed on same network

Choose ShareTexts if…

Devices are on different networks, you need text transfer, or you want a simpler pairing flow with codes and QR.

Choose PairDrop if…

Both devices are on the same local network, you want self-hosting control, or you need offline transfers without internet.
