About

What is ShareText?

ShareText lets you move text, photos, and files from one device to another — right in your browser. No app to install, no account to make.

In one line: Open sharetexts.online on both devices, connect them, send anything. Close the tab when you're done — nothing is kept.

Why people use it

It just works — iPhone to Windows, Android to Mac, any mix. If both devices have a browser, you're set.

It's private — your files go directly between the two devices, encrypted. The server never sees them.

It leaves nothing behind — no accounts, no history on our servers. When the room closes, it's gone.

How to use it

  1. Open it on both devices

    Go to sharetexts.online in any browser.

  2. Connect the devices

    Tap Send on one and Receive on the other, then use the 6-digit code, the QR code, the link — or just tap the other device when it shows up under Nearby.

  3. Send anything

    Type, paste, or attach. It appears on the other device instantly.

  4. Done

    Disconnect, close the tab, and everything disappears. Nothing is stored.

What you can send

Text, links, photos, videos, audio, documents — any file your browser can pick. If it fits on your device, you can send it.

Which devices work

Any two of these: iPhone, iPad, Android phone or tablet, Windows PC, Mac, Linux computer. You need a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) and internet on both devices.

Is it safe?

Yes. Everything is encrypted end-to-end while it travels between your devices. We never see your messages or files, and nothing is saved on our servers. There's no sign-up, so there's nothing to leak.
