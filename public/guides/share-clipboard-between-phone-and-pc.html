How to Share Your Clipboard Between Phone and PC

Copy a link on your phone, paste it into your PC's browser — or move text the other way — without any app, account, or cable.

Quick answer: Open sharetexts.online in the browsers of both devices, connect them once, then paste what you copied and press Enter. It lands on the other device instantly, ready to paste where you need it.

Step by Step

  1. Open ShareTexts on both devices

    Any browser works — Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone, Edge on Windows, anything on your Mac or Linux machine. Nothing to install.

  2. Connect the two devices

    Tap Send on one device and enter the 6-digit code on the other. If both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, you can simply tap its name when it appears under Nearby. QR works too: tap Show QR on one device and scan it with the other's camera.

  3. Paste and send

    Long-press → Paste (or Ctrl+V) in the composer at the bottom. Press Enter. The text appears on the other device — tap it once to copy it there.

What People Move This Way

Links — The classic: an article open on your phone, a YouTube video, a Google Maps route — open it full-size on your computer.

One-time passwords and codes — A code texted to your phone that you need to type on your PC.

Wi-Fi passwords, addresses, account numbers — Small pieces of text that are painful to retype and none of any cloud sync service's business.

Commands and snippets — A terminal command from your phone's notes, or a code snippet from your PC to a chat on your phone.

Does This Work Between iPhone and Windows? Android and Mac?

Yes — that's the point. Vendor clipboard sync only works inside one ecosystem (Apple to Apple, via Universal Clipboard; Samsung to Samsung). ShareTexts is just a web page, so it works between any combination: iPhone to Windows PC, Android to MacBook, and everything else.

Is My Clipboard Text Private?

Your text travels directly between the two browsers you connected — it is not stored on a server and does not pass through any ShareTexts storage. The connection is end-to-end encrypted by the browser's WebRTC layer. When you're done, close the tab and the room disappears. See Privacy & Security for details.

Does the Clipboard Sync Both Ways Automatically?

No, and that's deliberate. Nothing on your device is read automatically — you choose what to send by pasting it and pressing Enter. That means no background service watching your clipboard, and nothing leaves a device until you say so.
