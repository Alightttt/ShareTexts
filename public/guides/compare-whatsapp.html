The "Email It to Yourself" Problem

People often use WhatsApp (or email) to send a file from one device to another. This works, but it's not ideal:

  • Compression — WhatsApp compresses photos, reducing quality
  • Account required — you need a phone number and the app installed
  • History — the file stays in your chat history forever
  • Server storage — files are stored on WhatsApp's servers
  • Two-step process — open app, find chat, send, switch devices, find chat, download

Where ShareText Is Useful

  • Quick self-transfer — faster than opening WhatsApp and finding a chat
  • Photo quality — original quality, no compression
  • Privacy — no account, no history, no server storage
  • No app needed — works on any device with a browser
  • Text/links — paste and send, appears on the other device

Where WhatsApp Is Better

  • You already have it — most people already have WhatsApp installed
  • Works offline — send now, receive when online
  • Large files — up to 2 GB per file
  • Persistent — files are available later if you need them again
  • Group sharing — send to multiple people at once

Choose ShareText if…

You want a fast, private, one-time transfer without compression, accounts, or history. Especially useful for photos you want at original quality.

Choose WhatsApp if…

You already have it installed, need to share with others, want files to persist, or need to send very large files (up to 2 GB).
