How to Transfer Files from Computer to Phone

Move a file, photo, document, or link from your computer to your phone — directly from the browser. No USB cable, no cloud upload.

Quick answer: Open sharetexts.online on both devices. Click Send on your computer, enter the code on your phone, select the file. Done.

Step by Step

  1. Open ShareText on both devices

    Go to sharetexts.online on your computer and your phone's browser.

  2. Click Send on your computer

    On your computer, click Send something. A 6-digit pairing code appears.

  3. Enter the code on your phone

    On your phone, tap Receive something and enter the code. Or scan the QR code from your computer screen.

  4. Select and send the file

    On your computer, click the attachment icon or drag a file into the composer. The file transfers to your phone.

  5. Save on your phone

    Your phone shows the received file. Tap Save to download it.

Why Not Email It to Yourself?

You could — but ShareText is faster. No waiting for email sync, no file size limits from your email provider, no login. The file moves directly between your two devices.

Supported File Types

  • Photos and videos (PNG, JPG, WEBP, MP4, MOV)
  • Documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV)
  • Audio files (MP3, WAV, AAC)
  • Archives (ZIP, RAR)
  • Any file type your browser can select

Mac to iPhone Without AirDrop

If AirDrop isn't working or you need to send to a non-Apple device, ShareText works as a reliable alternative. No setup, no configuration — just open the browser and go.
