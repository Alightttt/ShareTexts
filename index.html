ShareTexts

Move anything between your devices.

ShareTexts sends text, links, photos, videos, and files directly between any two devices — phone to PC, iPhone to Windows — straight from your browser. No app, no account, no cable.

Send on one device, Receive on the other — free to use, no sign-up. How it works, below.

How ShareTexts works

  1. Open sharetexts.online on both devices — in the browser. Nothing to install.
  2. Find your other device. It appears in Nearby while ShareTexts is open on both devices; otherwise connect with a six-digit code, a QR code, or a share link.
  3. Connect with one tap. The room is end-to-end encrypted between your devices.
  4. Send anything — text, links, photos, videos, audio, any file. It moves directly between the devices and is never stored on a server.
  5. Receive it on the other device — then close the room. Nothing is kept.

Frequently asked questions

What is ShareTexts?

ShareTexts is a temporary bridge between two devices. Move text, links, photos, videos, and files directly from one screen to another. No app, no account, nothing kept.

Do I need to install an app or create an account?

No. ShareTexts runs in the browser — no app, no account, nothing to install. It works between an iPhone and a Windows PC, or any two devices with a modern browser.

What can I send?

Text, links, photos, videos, audio, documents — any file type, transferred as the original bytes. Transfers are end-to-end encrypted and never stored on a server.

How do devices find each other?

Four ways: Nearby discovery (the other device appears automatically while ShareTexts is open on both), a six-digit code, a QR code, or a share link. The code, QR, and link work over any internet connection.

Is Nearby discovery private?

Yes. Your device appears only while ShareTexts is open on it, and other devices see a temporary label like “Windows PC” — never your IP, accounts, or any permanent identifier.

Is ShareTexts private?

Transfers are encrypted between devices. ShareTexts keeps no account, no permanent history, and no copies of your content. Rooms close automatically.

What happens if my internet drops mid-transfer?

ShareTexts tells you whether the transfer can be retried. For large files, a stable connection is recommended.

Can I use ShareTexts between an iPhone and an Android phone?

Yes. ShareTexts works between any two devices with a modern browser — iPhone to Android, Android to Windows, iPhone to Mac.
